## Job Statement
When a small or medium African business wants to provide 24/7 customer support, I want an easy-to-deploy AI chat agent that handles common queries and surfaces leads, so I can reduce support costs and increase conversions without hiring more staff.

**Current Solution & Pain Points**
- Current: Manual agent response, email, or outsourced contact centers
- Pain: Slow response times, high cost, lost leads, inconsistent answers
- Consequence: Lower conversion rates, frustrated customers, increased churn

**Success Metrics**
- 80% queries auto-resolved
- Faster avg response (<3s perceived)
- Increased lead capture rate and demo sign-ups

**Primary Users**
- Product/marketing owners (non-technical)
- Developers integrating embed snippet or APIs
